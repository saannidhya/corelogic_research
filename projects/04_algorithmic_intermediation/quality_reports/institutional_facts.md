### A.8 Pricing technology (Zestimate)

- **Fact:** On Feb 25, 2021, Zillow began using the Zestimate itself as an initial cash offer in eligible markets ("Zillow Starts Making Cash Offers For the Zestimate"). | **Source:** https://zillow.mediaroom.com/2021-02-25-Zillow-Starts-Making-Cash-Offers-For-the-Zestimate ; https://www.cnn.com/2021/02/25/tech/zillow-zestimate-cash-offer | **Quote/figure:** release title and date. | **Status:** VERIFIED
- **Fact:** On June 15, 2021 Zillow launched the "neural Zestimate" (neural-network-based model). National median error rate: 1.9% for on-market homes; 6.9% for off-market homes (an improvement of nearly a full percentage point); Zestimate was an initial cash offer on ~900,000 eligible homes across 23 markets, with eligibility expected to increase ~30%. | **Source:** https://zillow.mediaroom.com/2021-06-15-Zillow-Launches-New-Neural-Zestimate,-Yielding-Major-Accuracy-Gains ; https://www.zillow.com/tech/building-the-neural-zestimate/ | **Quote/figure:** "Zestimate's national median error rate for off-market homes is now 6.9%... - **Fact:** The on-market vs off-market error asymmetry (1.9% vs 6.9%) is the key institutional detail: iBuyer offers price *off-market* homes, where algorithmic error is ~3.6x larger. | **Source:** same as above (interpretation is ours) | **Quote/figure:** 1.9% vs 6.9%. | **Status:** VERIFIED (figures; framing is author interpretation)
