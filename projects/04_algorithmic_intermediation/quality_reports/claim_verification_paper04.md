## Discrepancies requiring regeneration

- **C22** — Draft says the US housing stock is worth "roughly $45 trillion (2024-2026 estimates)." Credible current estimates: **$49.7-50 trillion (Redfin, year-end 2024)** and **$55.1 trillion (Zillow Research, June 2025)**; the paper's likely anchor citation (Buchak, Matvos, Piskorski & Seru, NBER w28252 rev. June 2025, p. 2) describes the US housing market as **"valued at $50 trillion."** The $45T figure matches roughly 2022-vintage estimates, not 2024-2026. Recommended fix: "roughly $50 trillion" (cite Redfin Dec-2024 or Buchak et al.), or "$50-55 trillion" if citing Zillow's 2025 figure.
